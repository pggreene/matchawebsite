Our Story

Ogawa Matcha began as a small passion project among friends who grew up near Kyoto’s terraced tea fields.

Today, we work with farmers who use time-honored cultivation and stone-grinding techniques to preserve flavor and color.

From seed to cup — a mindful, careful journey intended to bring quiet luxury to your day.
